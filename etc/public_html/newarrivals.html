For New Arrivals

Welcome to Nashville! Whether you have just landed or are still finding your feet, Friends of South Africa is here to help you settle into life in middle-Tennessee. This page gathers local resources, support organisations and community connections to help new South African arrivals get familiar with Nashville and feel at home.

You are not alone — reach out, come to an event, and let our community help you build your new life here.

Local Resources & Support

Nashville International Center for Empowerment (NICE) has helped refugees and immigrants find housing, work and belonging in Middle Tennessee since 2005. They offer English classes, youth programs, immigration support, healthcare navigation and more.

Visit Nashville International Center for Empowerment (NICE)

South African Consulate in New York

We appreciate the work of our South African Consulate in New York! They are your point of contact for passports, official documents and consular services while you are in the United States.
